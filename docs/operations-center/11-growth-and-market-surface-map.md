## Positioning evidence

The site positions Pourdex as predictive inventory intelligence for independent
bars/restaurants, differentiated by POS integration, recipe mapping, variance,
forecasting, ordering, and margin intelligence.

Confidence: high for current copy; market fit/differentiation unverified.
Impact if wrong: high. Validation: customer/prospect research and current
competitor sources.
